# Folio management panel

Mode: Operate. Preserve the existing Folio identity and Paper Library inspired dense reading workflow.

A solid white local WebView holds a three pane library: nested folders, compact paper rows, bibliographic detail. Muted green controls, blue gray document type icons, green rank badges and violet impact factor badges. System text, 11–13 px metadata, single line paper titles, 78 px rows. No personal star rating; stars are favorites. Both sidebars resize at their inner dividers and can collapse. Paper row hover and selection backgrounds include their compact action group. Window corner drags change dimensions without multiplying pointer movement. Narrow views show either list or detail. Filter and sort menus match the trigger width and appear directly below it; types are multi-select. Settings occupies a separate full page. Native plugin storage remains the persistent source of truth.
